## 1. THE "100 % CALIDAD" DECAL, SQUARE ON AND CLOSE  *(NEW — highest value for the artwork)*

**Why we cannot settle it here.** `ref_playa_34.png` is the **only frame in the entire set that shows
this decal** (`REFERENCE_FRAMES_rev45.md` §2), and the burst spans **23 × 39 px** in it. The letters
are 4–6 px tall.

**What it would settle, all at once:**

* **"The lettering looks off as well"** — his report. Nothing about a typeface is measurable at 4 px.
  Rev 46 declined to invent a number for it.
* **"The bottom word does not collide with the top one either"** — confirmed in the build (the two
  words share **1110 pixels**) but we have no photographed target for the gap.
* the burst's spike count, their length ratio and the jitter pattern;
* whether the pink star is where we put it, and its size;
* the type's angle, currently −19.7° from a measurement taken at this same 23 px.

A phone photo taken square on to the panel from two metres would settle every one of these.

## 2. THE REAR PANEL, SQUARE ON, SHOWING THE VENT SLATS  *(NEW)*

He identified the two dark lines above the Calidad decal as **vent slats**, and he is right — they
are dark grey in the photograph while the model was painting them saturated red into the decal
texture. They are now removed from the artwork.

**The model has no rear vents at all.** `grep -rn 'vent|louvre|louver|slat|intake'` over every source
returns the cab door's quarter-light and the studio lighting rig, and nothing else.

**What we need to build them rather than guess:** the **count**, the **pitch**, each slat's **length**,
and their **height above the decal**. None is measurable at the resolution we hold.
