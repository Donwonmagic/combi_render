## 7. ONE HUBCAP, SQUARE ON AND CLOSE — **the only new ask**

**Why it is needed.** He reported at rev 51: *"The vw on the hubcap is not the right scale either,
just like the front emblem."* The hubcap badge is built from two constants and **only one of them
has ever been compared to a photograph**:

* the **DIAMETER**, `CAP_EMBLEM_D` — compared, and the diameter route on `ref_side.jpg` is
  **EXHAUSTED**. Rev 51 calibrated a synthetic emblem at known ratio and known blur at that frame's
  own scale and got a true emblem/cap of **0.3474** against the built **0.3170** — 9.6 % small but
  only **1.8 sigma** against the record's own declared +-0.017. Re-running it returns the same
  1.8 sigma. **Do not re-run it.**
* the **STROKE WEIGHT**, `CAP_EMBLEM_WFRAC = 0.2087` — **never compared to any frame.** It declares
  its own provenance in the source: `# w/R as authored (0.0072 / 0.0345), kept`, and **0.0345 was
  the OLD emblem radius** — rev 14's, which rev 15 found at 7.0 sigma and corrected to 0.3170. The
  diameter was fixed and **the stroke ratio was kept from the wrong-sized object.**

**What is needed:** one frame of a single hubcap, **square on and close** — the badge filling a
useful part of the frame, lit so the chrome is not blown.

**Why no frame we hold will do, and these routes are CLOSED — do not re-try them blind:**
* every frame we hold puts the badge at **~19 px**, on a red dome or on chrome;
* `IMG_2073.jpeg` by automated mask — rev 51 tried it: **chrome is hostile to threshold
  segmentation.** The "cap" mask selected the painted rim and the "emblem" mask a dark environment
  reflection;
* `ref_workshop.jpg` — useless here: **both road wheels are bare rims with NO HUBCAP fitted**;
* `ref_side.jpg` for the nose stroke width — the rev-49 survey dropped it as *"too oblique to
  measure a perpendicular width from"*.

**What one frame would settle:** the emblem DIAMETER and the STROKE WEIGHT on the part he has just
reported, **and** it would confirm the seated dome's proudness directly (rev 51 recovered
52.2 mm +- 7 by a shape-free estimator and seated the cap to 58.2 mm, 0.8 sigma).
