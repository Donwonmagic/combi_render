## 1. THE SPINE

**IT STOPPED, AND WE MEASURED IT.**

Two facts, and each one rescues the other.

**The object stopped.** SPEC §0.1 carries Tacombi's own account at grade S: *"The engine was scrapped, transmission sold, seats swapped for cooking equipment, and the roof was reeled back."* The entire VW-bus merchandise market — sunset, surf, road, escape — sells motion. Ours cannot move. That is not a limitation to design around; it is the only unoccupied emotional position in a saturated category, and it is factually ours alone. It dictates everything downstream: the motion vocabulary is DEPLOYMENT, never driving. The compositions are planted, asymmetric and OPEN — lid up, counter out, tail board propped — because that is the shape people recognise and the shape no stock asset has. And the anti-derivative content is *the conversion, not the vehicle*: everything above the sill and behind the cab door.

**And the asset is a survey, not a render.** After 76 revisions this project holds 229 named objects, 0 non-manifold body edges, 43 of 45 materials bound — and, uniquely, **a graded ledger of everything it failed to establish.** SPEC §0 already carries the vocabulary (M measured / S sourced / R research / E inferred / U unresolved). Nobody else on earth has either half.

The formal consequence — and this is what makes it a body of work rather than a pile of posters — is that **this asset can do exactly two things no photograph and no stock library can: SUBTRACT and DECLARE.** It can remove what is actually there (the paint off the flank, the people off the terrace, the artwork off the panel, the light off the string). And it can state, on the face of the work, how well each thing is known — including where the honest answer is *it cannot be recovered from what we hold.*

So the program's signature move is not photorealism. It is a measured object that publishes its own doubt. Every line below is one register of that: the survey, the conversion, the hand, the keepsake.

**The corollary that governs every crop:** we escape VW by drawing what Volkswagen never made, and we escape the render's own open defects — the salmon red, the 0.412 gloss — by working in flat ink and line, where neither gate applies. Those are the same decision.

*Cold, technical, unfakeable. What only a measured object can say.*

**1. SHEET 1 OF 4 — THE UNCERTAINTY PLATE.** A2 orthographic side elevation where every dimension carries its evidence grade, and the unknowable ones are drawn as **tinted bands to scale, not written as caveats** — a 36-unit band from 48 to 84 lugs; an open-ended band for TB_WIDTH running off the sheet edge with `UPPER BOUND 590. **2. NO LEGIBLE.** Three sheets. Every specimen the camera could not read, reproduced **at 1:1 with the vehicle** — so legibility on the paper is the legibility a customer standing at the counter actually gets, and the failure is the camera's. The hero is a hole: 695 rectified px of palm-frond occlusion = 589 mm of untouched stock in a flooded sheet, captioned `THAT IS A CONSISTENCY, NOT A READING`. *Earns its place:* the corpus was written by build scripts recording failures, not composed for a poster; a stock illustrator would have supplied the missing thirteen characters. 1:1 is also unavailable to every gallery precedent it resembles, because none of them has a measured object behind the photograph.

**3. THE SAME CAMERA.** A three-panel plate: the founding photograph; the camera solve drawn on it in one hairline; the same frame rebuilt in that photograph's own measured light with the terrace still furnished and the afternoon subtracted. The middle panel carries a single tick at column 555 — `PREDICTED 554.6. READ 555. — a corner the solve was never shown.` *Earns its place:* a held-out validation is the difference between a reconstruction and a fit, and almost nobody in forensic viz publishes one. The absence rule is legible in two seconds — **what is bolted on stays, what was set down that afternoon goes** — which is what separates elegiac from unfinished.

### LINE 2 — THE CONVERSION
*Marks and systems containing no Volkswagen at all.*

**4. TRES HUECOS — the aperture system.** Three equal 515.5 × 403.0 mm openings on **unequal** 625.0 / 645.0 mm centres, so the pillars are 109.5 and 129.5 mm. That asymmetry is the fingerprint: a factory bus has equal glass; ours has three holes somebody cut by eye. Runs as wallcovering, packaging, textile, tile and a laser-cut screen. *Earns its place:* it is the brand's founding fact (*"missing three of its windows"*) as a repeat, it contains no vehicle, and at small scale it reads as pattern first and reference second — which is the whole answer to the walking-advert problem.

**5. THE FILAMENT.** The festoon as one continuous drawn path — drip rail, rear dome, tail board — plus a second run down the T1 V-swage so it reads as a vehicle rather than a bar. One ink, animates for free, survives to a favicon. *Earns its place:* it is the light the bus makes, not the bus; the record's own F144 says the render fails it, so the 2D piece is correct where the 3D is measurably stuck.

**6. 1963 / THE BRASS LINE.** Micro-goods off the empty chrome plate frame and the counter's brass nosing: enamel pin, hem tag, coaster, woven label, matchbook. *Earns its place:* the owner dated the object on its own back. Zero trademark surface, 25–40 mm, cheapest thing in the program, and the entry point that teaches everything above it.

### LINE 3 — THE HAND
*Rotulismo. The artwork being made, and the artwork itself.*

**7. ROTULISTA.** A locked orthographic film in which a bare red panel is painted onto in measured order — three masses first (~78 % of the drawing in two seconds), then the dark commas, then tendrils and rosettes at a reload rhythm, then the hundred-odd remaining marks in one frame worth 2.29 % of the ink, then the lettering in three passes. Hard cut to the working bus. The **progression plate** (four band strips at the census's own inflection points) is the better artefact and should lead. *Earns its place:* it reframes the florals from Woodstock to Mexican signwriting, which only motion can do; and its opening frame — this bus with its paint removed — is a photograph that cannot be taken.

**8. EL TABLERO.** The flower mural lid as flat art at full bleed: eleven stylised heads on orange stems over red scrollwork, framed in olive-yellow menu strips lettered in dark red. No render, no camera, no chain — it is a 4096 × 2476 master on disk. *Earns its place:* it is the loudest, warmest, most saturated graphic the brand owns, it is the only place its display typography appears at scale, and it is the single cheapest high-quality poster/tote/bandana available. Blocked only by F74(a), a one-generator fix.

### LINE 4 — THE KEEPSAKE
*Families, children, things people keep. All three are risky and I am proposing them anyway.*

**9. EL COMBI — LA LOTERÍA. ⚠ RISKY.** 24 engraved cards, every one an object that exists in the mesh, each stamped with how well it is known — ▣ MEDIDO / ◆ DICTADO / ○ POSTURA / ✕ NO CONSTA. Four cards say plainly that nobody knows. 3×3 tablas so the game actually plays. *Earns its place:* it is the only idea that turns 229 measured objects and a ledger of refusals into a thing a family keeps in a drawer. **The risk is real:** custom lotería is a saturated Etsy category and a high-school art assignment; the differentiator is the stamps and the cutaway cards, not the format; and it needs a bespoke hatch engine plus 24 finished illustrations before one unit ships.

**10. SE PRENDE. ⚠ RISKY.** Deli paper printed both faces: on top the bus open, rigged and completely DARK; underneath, amber, only the light — with a hidden oil-barrier resist that channels grease along the vehicle's own service path, so the counter lights first, then the hatches, then the galley, then the rail, and it stops where you stopped eating. *Earns its place:* the taqueria's highest-volume printed object, a substrate no studio touches, and the brand's proposition expressed as a physical property of paper. **The risk is real and testable in week one:** greaseproof is refined to do the opposite of what this needs, the resist behaviour is unverified, and if the lanes do not hold oil it collapses into a known grease-reveal wrapper.

**11. AQUÍ ME QUEDO. ⚠ RISKY.** Wide, low, warm: the bus as the only motionless, hard-edged, fully-coloured thing while everything around it moves — with a tended herb in a chipped tin on the counter shelf. *Earns its place:* it is the spine as a picture, and it is the only piece in the program with warmth a child reads instantly. **The risk:** overgrown-vehicle art is an existing merch genre whose meaning is DECAY, which on a food brand is a hygiene adjacency — hence the tin, not the volunteer weed through the arch.

## 4. THE CRITICAL PATH

*Ordered by leverage per minute. Items 1–4 are the enabling layer for four of the eleven concepts.*

**1. RUN `T1_ALPHA=1` ONCE. ~6 min.** Thirteen revisions un-run; no LEDGER contains the string; every frame in `out/` is colortype 2 and `deliver.py` correctly prints `PACKAGE IS NOT TRUSTWORTHY`. One env var unblocks die-cut, patches, compositing, and `layers/*_no_shadow` + `*_shadow_only` — which are the two inputs to the owner's own lost rev-39 clause, *"vector line and flat colour, shading and occlusion sampled from the 3D asset."* **Do not route it through `post.py`:** measured, RGBA in, colortype 2 out. Optics and cut-out are mutually exclusive and nothing documents it.

**2. THE ORTHO-BORDER GUARD. ~30 min, and it is a watched kill for free.** Assert zero ink on all four borders of any ortho frame. It goes RED on `r75_front` (T42/B738), `r75_hero34f` (L563/B265) and `r75_hero34r` (R493), and GREEN on `r75_side` (0/0/0/0) — rule 3 satisfied without inventing a defect. Then fix `front`/`rear`: `ortho=3.55` with horizontal fit covers z 0.2997–2.7403 against a `bbox top 3.132`. Derive from `build.py`'s printed bbox with a stated margin.

**3. THE LINE PASS GO/NO-GO. ~1 s, then one real-mesh run.** `grep -rniE 'line_art|freestyle|grease|gpencil'` across every `.py` returns **nothing** — no line has ever been extracted from this asset. `GreasePencilLineartModifier` and `wm.grease_pencil_export_svg` both exist in this bpy; the question is whether they survive 366,490 faces with 5,186 ngons on a shade-smooth shell. **If vector: A2/A1 are resolution-free and Lines 1, 2 and 4 are all buildable.** If not, the fallback is Sobel over normal + Z + object-index AOVs, which caps print at ~A2/164 dpi. Sweep crease angle 30–40° and LOOK; lower finds the subdivision.

**4. THE PASS KIT.** Normal, AO, object/material index (driven from `build.py`'s own 17 `A(obj, key)` assignments), cryptomatte, Z. Copy `matte_tap`'s colour contract verbatim — `OVERRIDE / Raw / None / gamma 1.0` — or every pass returns AgX-curved. **Two corrections to the obvious recipe:** use the SHADING normal, not True Normal (`probe_rev55_truenorm` says True Normal fires on facet boundaries — tessellation, not folds); and do NOT copy its `color_depth='8'`, which is a PIL workaround. Write 16-bit, read with `photometry.read_png`. Watched kill: delete the vehicle, confirm object index identically zero.

**5. `T1_SAVE` PERSISTENCE + EXPORT.** `build.py` carries `bpy.ops.wm.save_as_mainfile` and it appears in **zero** handoff documents. Save one `.blend`, run every isolation and variant from it. Then glTF/USD — and name the real blocker: **the flank art is BOX-PROJECTED in the shader and box projection does not export.** It must be baked to a UV set first, and UV/ngon survival is untested.

**6. LIVERY ABLATIONS.** Only `T1_W_ART` exists and it gates the folk art alone. `script_L`/`script_R` and `calidad_L` need independent switches for ROTULISTA's bare panel. Ablate each, render, **crop and look** — a ghost at low alpha reads as a smudge and no gate will say so (rule 47).

**7. `T1_VW_MARK` + AN IN-FRAME TRADEMARK PROBE.** Three states: ship / mark-neutral repaint / not built. All hooks exist (`_EMBLEM_PLATE`, the `capvw` tag, materials `roundelred` and `capwhite`). Then a probe that projects every `capvw*`, `vw_disc`, `vw_ring`, `vwbar` vertex through a camera and crop and asserts zero in-crop pixels — watched failing on the uncropped frame. **This matters more than it looks:** a side elevation carries four VW glyphs at 12,438 px², and `STATE.md` says of exactly that mark that its width fraction *"has never been compared to a frame."*

**8. TEXTURE REPAIRS — AND ONE REFUSAL THAT THREE EXPERT PLANS GOT WRONG.**
- **F74(a):** `tex/lidmural.png` is cropped at the bottom. Regenerate **by hand** — `lid_gen.py` is not called by `build.py`.
- **F16:** the pink star is built below the Calidad burst where both red frames put it above; rosettes are drawn at the diameter of their gold core.
- **DO NOT add a keyline to `script_gen.py`.** SPEC §10.16 forbids it verbatim: *"Do not add a keyline to the generator because sec.3 says so; that is the same error as building from the thumbnail."* Its sampling shows monotone decay with no dark ring — edge blur, not a keyline. Three separate expert plans call this "the cheapest visible improvement in the inventory"; **it is forbidden by the record.** I re-measured the asset: among alpha>200 pixels the minimum luma is 145.7 and the fraction below luma 80 is exactly 0.0000. The legal route is §10.16's own remedy — **add a PHOTOS_WANTED item: one close, square-on frame of the side wordmark.**
- **And retract §10.16's other half in the same edit.** It claims the asset *"emits a constant (214, 216, 218) with all shape in alpha."* Live, I measured per-channel std 14.15 / 15.95 / 17.45 across 4,083 distinct ink colours, against the photograph's measured 16–19. **The flatness complaint is discharged; the keyline one is not.**
- The wordmark's **placement** is separately fixable and not gated by §10.16: `flank_compare` puts it +76.2 mm forward and −47.6 mm down, cross-checked at +83/+80 mm aft.

**9. DO NOT RE-ANGLE THE TAIL BOARD.** F242 refutes F165's 28.0°: `ref_side.jpg` — the primary frame, current livery — images it at 38.8° against the shipped 38.0, a 0.8° match, while `IMG_3840` images the same board at 21.0°. **The angle is a pose that varies by day, and two prior briefs instructed a change that would have made the model worse.** What is genuinely unmeasured: the board's angle on `ref_rear34.jpg`, the frame concept 3 registers against.

**10. LOWER PRIORITY, NAMED SO NOBODY REDISCOVERS IT.** `LID_W = (LID_X0−LID_X1)/LID_ASPECT/sin(LID_OPEN_DEG)` inflates the mural board to 6.84 m at 10° and raises `ZeroDivisionError` at 0°, with a **module-scope assert** that kills every importer with a bare traceback. Every shortlisted concept now draws the bus OPEN, so this is deferred — freeze behind `T1_LIDW_FREEZE` only when a shut or swept lid is actually needed, and watch the assert fire at 45° first. Likewise `F57` (`T1_SCENE=playa` renders no vehicle) is time-boxed and has never been run on `playa_ref` — one frame settles whether it even reproduces there.

## 7. RISKS

**FIDELITY CEILINGS.** Nothing above 1600×1100 exists on disk and `out/` is untracked, so every large frame ever made is gone. The large-format chain has been proven once, at 3840×2640, at rev 57, 106.8 min for one view — and it is **8-bit end to end**: `studio.py` writes `color_depth = '16'` and both `stitch.py` and `post.py` read through PIL, which has no 16-bit RGB path and truncates silently. On a metre-wide print that is banding in a large flat cream field, and nobody has looked. `hq_render.py` also carries no `T1_FX=0` where the chain it replaced forced it off to stop per-strip optics banding at the seams.

**THE RED CANNOT CURRENTLY BE ADJUDICATED, AND THAT IS A RESULT.** `flank_compare` reads the render at G/R 0.422 against the photograph's 0.114 — it images salmon. But F258's verdict is *"NEITHER CONSTANT IS WRONG, THE SCENE IS"*: the render is a white studio, the photograph is outdoor shade, and the comparison is confounded. So **every flat-ink artefact must DECLARE its red as a design ruling with stated provenance, never sample it** — not from a render, and not by averaging the photographs, where `probe_rev71_red.py` prints its own prohibition (*"0.0149 .. 0.0344, a 2.3x span. DO NOT QUOTE A SINGLE FIGURE"*). Three authored reds already disagree.

**PUBLISHING WHAT THE PROJECT CANNOT DEFEND.** `TREAD_LUGS = 64` is a declaration inside a measured 48..84 bracket that the probe refuses to collapse — at poster scale the lug pitch is ~4.9 mm and countable. The hubcap badge's width fraction has never been compared to any frame. The tail board's chord has three irreconcilable values (0.710 / 0.790 / 0.829) with an explicit instruction not to average them. **Draw the bracket or draw nothing.**

**IP.** VW enforces against merchandise and claims the bus shape itself; "given away rather than sold" is the weakest available defence, since promotional distribution by a trading business is use in commerce. A side elevation carries four hubcap roundels. The mitigations are structural and free — crop the conversion, blank the cap dome, decline the glyph — but the posture question is the owner's and a lawyer's, not a designer's.

**WHERE IT COULD DISAPPOINT.** Three ways, in order of likelihood.

*First:* **this program is heavily 2D and derived, and the owner has been told for four revisions that geometry is not shipping.** If the whole thing lands as posters, it reads as the project changing the subject. Mitigation: item 5 of the critical path (export) and item 6 (livery ablations) are genuine model capabilities, and ROTULISTA's bare-panel frame is a visible change to the vehicle. Say plainly which artefacts are derived and which are the model getting better.

*Second:* **printing uncertainty can read as excuse-making.** The bands and the NOT ISSUED sheets are the program's best idea and its most fragile. The defence is register: **biography is the hero and epistemology is the reward for leaning in.** `ENGINE: NONE. TRANSMISSION: SOLD.` stops a stranger at four feet; the parallax bounds are for the reader who has two minutes. If pushed, concede the bounds and defend the biography.

*Third:* **nobody in this program has seen a single piece of current Tacombi merchandise, brand guideline, official colour or logo lockup.** Every first-party page returned EGRESS_BLOCKED; the typeface attribution (Cooper Black / Davida / Bookman) is a second-hand summary of a page nobody opened. **Do not state a Tacombi brand colour or typeface as fact in any document.** One browser session on his side closes it.

And two facts to keep visible: `deliver.py` writes its full 21-file package and a README claiming transparent backgrounds **even in the run that prints `PACKAGE IS NOT TRUSTWORTHY`** — and that README tells the owner his emblem *"reads as an X"* and has been *"reported six times"* when it is a V over a W and his ninth report, names a tyre tread that shipped at rev 74, and names roof loudspeakers retired at rev 74. **Fix it before any package leaves the building.** Finally, the *"earlier cartoon version"* he praised is in none of 793 commits and the directory `START_HERE.md` points at does not exist here — do not promise to match a drawing nobody in this program can see.

## What I changed, and what I refused

**Grafted in and pushed further**

- **Purged the archetypes, and verified the replacements against `STATE.md`.** El Sol is gone (it is the most-reproduced lotería card, and `AUDIT_rev43.md` already rules "THE SUN — it must NOT be a rayed disc"). **La Bocina is gone** — F309 retires the roof loudspeakers as "REMOVABLE EVENT GEAR… a POSE, not vehicle geometry," and no speaker object exists in the inventory. **El Vaso is gone**, not renamed to `gal_sqbottle`: loose galley dressing is F309's own failure class. Replacements are all in the live inventory — **El Volante** (`wheel_rim`/`wheel_hub`/`wheel_horn`/`wheel_spoke|2`), **El Reloj** (`speedo_bezel`/`speedo_face`/`speedo_glass`), **El Gozne** (`hinge_barrel|4`/`hinge_leaf|4`). **La Llave stays as `gal_warmer_tap`** — verified at `A(_gcyl("gal_warmer_tap", ...))`.
- **The measured-figure footer, transformed.** Printing dimensions is right; printing *these* dimensions was not. I refused every figure the record cannot defend with one ruler: the chord (F245 leaves 0.710 / 0.790 / 0.829 and says "Do not average them"), the badge Ø (`STATE.md`: "hubcap badge is SELF-CONSISTENCY ONLY — CAP_EMBLEM_WFRAC has never been compared to a frame"), the bay width (0.516 / 0.515 / 0.516), and 4.580 m (flagged "+525.0 mm OUT", while the same file's verify line reads 4.317 with lids / 4.065 without). What replaced it is **the four-stamp provenance system** — MEDIDO / DICTADO / POSTURA / NO CONSTA. Four cards now *print a refusal*. That is the deck's best idea and it came out of the constraint, not around it.
- **Game math fixed without either auditor's remedy.** 3 × 3 tablas of nine, eight of them: mean shared cards between boards is k²/N = 81/24 = 3.4 of 9, against 256/24 = 10.7 of 16 for the proposed 4 × 4.
- **A visible proof of origin.** Card 1 is a partial cutaway, card 2 hatches through the aperture onto the galley. The normals argument is true but imperceptible; the cutaway is perceptible.
- **The hatch engine promoted to the headline of the work**, with the sphere/plane known-answer validation, and **two corrections that would each have cost a revision**: `probe_rev55_truenorm.py`'s own message says *"THE TRUE NORMAL IS NOT THE FIX… fires on every FACET boundary — tessellation, not folds"* (both the concept and an auditor cited it for the opposite), and `matte_tap`'s `color_depth='8'` is a PIL workaround that must not be copied to a density pass.
- **Card 24 re-argued rather than re-specified.** The `LID_W`/angle coupling isn't an obstacle to route around — it is wrong physics. A panel's width is a property of the panel; the aspect 1.7130 is a *projected* reading at one propped angle. Freezing `LID_W` is the correct derivation, and it has grounding: F309 records that in `ref_playa_34.png` "the board is down and the bus is closed."
- Also taken: `T1_SAVE` build-once/isolate-23, Line Art measured on the real mesh first, geometric La Sombra, the cropped louvre run, the dichos and the displaced-archetype line, the box stating the category error, the stated scale, the not-the-sticker framing, the three-card prototype, the four-density no-blend rule as the anti-scratchboard signature.
- **Corrections to the concept's own facts:** La Flor is **eight** lobes and eight petal-dots (`lid_gen.py`: "LOBE COUNT — rev 10's TWELVE IS REFUTED… N_LOBE = 8"); ten is the number of *heads*. The 84.4 % gold figure is the **photograph's**; the built figure is 81.0 %.

**Refused, with reasons**

1. **La Combi Verde.** The *facts* are grade S in SPEC §0.1 (green, $3,000, "missing three of its windows", "only had the front bench"). The *identification* is not: §0.1 says "This is the Playa bus, NOT the Nolita one", the carriers list `ref_workshop.jpg` under "NOT the target, geometry only", and SPEC records the green-vs-red attribution as still contested. There is no frame of the Playa bus as bought and no geometry for it. Drawing it would publish a position on an open identity question and invent an unbuilt vehicle — "it cannot be recovered from what we hold."
2. **Going to 54 cards.** It triples the illustration cost to solve a problem a 3 × 3 tabla solves for free, and the conceit does not improve with dilution.

## What I changed, and what I refused

**The single structural change: the bus is now drawn OPEN and DARK, not shut and dark.** This was auditor 3's lead improvement and it pays four ways at once — it deletes the shut-state build entirely (and with it the `LID_W` 1/sin blocker, the pose rows a shut build would red, and the whole unscoped ablation family), it removes the dry sheet from the two stock tropes the market survey documents, it makes the dry state carry the brand at the 90 %+ of handovers that never get wet, and it keeps the only transition that means anything: DARK → LIT.

**The single creative escalation: the resist is now the idea, not an afterthought.** Auditor 1 proposed barrier lanes to structure the wick; I made them the vehicle's own service path so the lights come on in the order a taqueria wakes up — counter, hatches, galley, rail — and made the sequence-vs-blob control test the gate the concept is judged on. **What I changed, and what I refused**

**The three moves that did the most work — none of them was on an auditor's list:**

1. **Master frame moved from `hero34r` to the orthographic `side`.** `studio.py` reads `"side": dict(..., lens=None, ortho=5.90)`. ## What I changed, and what I refused

**The three auditor findings I verified and took whole.** The date is invented — `_getexif()` on `ref_rear34.jpg` returns nothing and SPEC's only dates are the purchase and the 6 Feb 2006 opening; every year is deleted from the title, labels and caption. **Three auditor claims did not survive the register, and I replaced rather than obeyed them.**

1. *"The tail board is ~10° too steep, and that kills 'nothing slides'"* — the producibility auditor's top fatal flaw. **F242 (MEASURED-rev70) refutes it**: `ref_side.jpg` images the board at 38.8° against the shipped 38.0, a 0.8° match, and building to F165's 28.0 "would have made the model WORSE." The auditor quoted `HANDOFF_CARRIERS.md` §0.05, which is pre-rev-70 prose; `OPEN_FINDINGS.md` outranks it. The *true* finding is better: F242 shows the board is a pose that varies 18° between frames, and **nobody has ever measured it on `ref_rear34.jpg`** — the frame this plate registers against. That became the item.
2. *"Resolve the three-way disagreement about which face carries the mural."* `t1_shell.py` and SPEC §4 already agree (underside, facing the counter), and `t1_shell.py` names `ref_rear34.jpg` as one of the three frames that arrangement reproduces. Only `lid_gen.py`'s comment dissents, and `lid_gen.py` isn't called by `build.py`. Demoted to "correct a stale comment," and replaced with SPEC 10.26's genuinely open question — what the second flowered panel in *this frame's* top right actually is.
3. *"Grade the print to handle the salmon red."* **Refused outright.** F258's verdict is verbatim: *"NEITHER CONSTANT IS WRONG, THE SCENE IS."* Its objection to every red measurement the project has made is that the photograph is outdoors-in-shade and the render is a white studio — *different scenes*. This concept is the only proposal that removes exactly that objection. So the format's apparent worst liability is actually the project's first admissible colour adjudication, and a cosmetic grade would destroy the one claim the plate makes. That inversion is now the concept's strongest technical argument.

**What I pushed past the auditors.** The absence rule: both auditors saw that a patchy subtraction reads as unfinished, but their proposed law ("everything measured, nothing carried in") fails — the speakers *were* measured and go; the yellow sign *was* measured and stays. **1. F18 IS ABSENT FROM AN ELEVEN-ITEM SHORTLIST, AND SO IS THE ONE TECHNIQUE THE OWNER ACTUALLY SPECIFIED.**

**2. THE LINE-2 ANCHOR PUBLISHES A FIGURE BELOW ITS OWN MEASUREMENT FLOOR — IN A PROGRAM WHOSE SPINE IS PUBLISHING DOUBT.**
TRES HUECOS states 109.5 and 129.5 mm as fact and calls the 20 mm asymmetry "the fingerprint." `t1_shell.py` line 136 states the source uncertainty in the same comment block: *"+0.672 / +0.047 / −0.598 **+/- 0.015**."* A pitch is a difference of two centres; the asymmetry is a difference of two pitches, σ ≈ 30 mm. **20.0 ± 30.0 is 0.67σ.** Three equal pillars sit inside the measurement. So the program's one identity system is anchored on a sub-floor difference, uncaveated, while §1 declares the signature move is "a measured object that publishes its own doubt." An adversary finds this in four minutes and the whole spine goes with it. Either re-measure the pillars **directly** as edge-to-edge gaps on one frame (common-mode error cancels; 20 mm is 4.4 px at `ref_side.jpg`'s 220 px/m), or draw three equal pillars and move the fingerprint to the door-to-bay-0 pillar, which has a stated *cause* rather than a residual.
