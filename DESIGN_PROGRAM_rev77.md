## §3 THE TWO COMPLETENESS CRITICS, RUN BEFORE THE SYNTHESIS

§9.2 asked for *"two completeness critics, not one, and run them BEFORE the synthesis as well as
after"*. **Both ran on the raw pool, so their findings could still change the outcome.** Verdicts:

**CRITIC 1:** BLUNT VERSION. You have fifty concepts and about eight objects, and you have not checked whether you are allowed to sell any of them. Fix that first: every commercially significant item here is a Volkswagen T1 silhouette or roundel, and in 77 revisions of a repository that measures a tail board to 0.1 mm there is not one line about trade dress. Only [dream:beautiful-3] spots it. The pivot is already on your own bench — TRES HUECOS — because the three serving apertures are Tacombi's cut, not Volkswagen's pressing, and they are the strongest measured fact you own. Move the mark off the vehicle and half your legal problem and all of your originality problem resolve at once.

BEST IDEA, BY NAME: [dream:beautiful-1] DIEZ FLORES Y MEDIA. It is the only thing here that is beautiful before it is clever, it monetises the one asset nobody at the counter can see, it dresses every table and sells as metre goods off one file, hule is the right cultural register and it is cheap, and flowers are not somebody else's trademark. Pay the painter or commission a taller — that cost is not negotiable and it is small. Runners-up that I would actually buy: [dream:free-7-2] the salsa bottle (the only repeat-revenue object in the pool; check the refill legality with an inspector on day one), [dream:free-5-1] the picture book (the only children's object anyone keeps for twenty years, and it delivers F18 inside its own back board), [dream:beautiful-2] the PROVECHO tin tray, [dream:la-combi-cerrada-2] the kraft bag.

WORST IDEA, BY NAME: [dream:packaging-2] DOS MANOS — the napkin printed with a non-consenting customer's face for the next customer to wipe grease on. The concept says it cannot tell whether that is moving or tasteless. It is tasteless, it is a likeness violation, and it is food-contact. Kill it. Most likely to become a news story: [dream:frontier-inside-neck-1] EL CARNET, a stamped border-document pastiche issued by a US restaurant group in 2026 — it names its own problem and then proposes to proceed with a caveat that will be cropped out of the first screenshot. Also kill [dream:twelve-shadows-1] (a chalk outline in a dining room) and [dream:photographic-3] (a forged photograph of a real trading business's vehicle).

PAY FOR AND KEEP: the cloth, the salsa, the book, the tray, the bag, the lotería deck already on your shortlist. ART SCHOOL: [dream:la-combi-cerrada-1], [dream:sound-2], [dream:frontier-inside-neck-3], [dream:photographic-1], [dream:people-1], [dream:free-8-3], [dream:motion-2], [dream:environmental-2], [dream:sound-1], [dream:garment-1], [dream:garment-2], [dream:garment-3]. WHAT A TAQUERIA NEEDS THAT IS NOT IN THIS POOL: a gift card and its carrier; a t-shirt, a cap and a tote; THE MENU; a shelf-ready retail food pack with a barcode; and the delivery seal plus the bag someone opens at home having never seen the room — your largest unbranded surface. You skipped the entire top of the revenue funnel and went straight to struck bronze medals and vitreous enamel tooling, and not one concept states a cost, a price, an MOQ or a lead time.

DERIVATIVE, NAMED: Thomas Ruff's jpegs ([dream:photographic-1]), Fontcuberta ([dream:photographic-3]), Smejkal's Fatescapes ([dream:people-1], [dream:free-8-3]), Dorothy's blueprint prints (the whole austere register, including THE UNCERTAINTY PLATE), Panini ([dream:free-6-1], [dream:packaging-3]), Margiela ([dream:garment-2]), Altiverb ([dream:sound-1]), the gift-shop VW lamp ([dream:beautiful-3]), the branded burger bun ([dream:free-1-1]), Muji-plus-Everlane for the confession-on-the-object house move.
