## 1. THE REAR PANEL, SQUARE ON — the vent slats, and a COUNT

**Partly answered by `IMG_2073.jpeg`, and this is the highest-value gap left.**

What we now have: the louvres at ~60 × 50 px on the rear flank, and a **measured pitch of
8.02 ± 0.42 native px** — regular to 5 %.

**What is still missing, and it is the number the geometry needs first:** the **COUNT**. A bounded
detector found **6** shadow lines in a 50-px crop that may not span the whole panel; reading the same
crop magnified by eye suggests **~10**. **Rev 47 refused to publish either.** Also unmeasured: each
slat's **length**, the panel's **height above the decal**, and whether the array is one panel or two.

Also settled by `IMG_2073` and worth recording: **the slats are BODY COLOUR, not dark grey.**
`LEDGER_rev46.md` §5 read them as dark grey from a frame where only the shadow survived. They are
green — the same paint as the panel — and read dark because **each pressed slot self-shadows**. That
makes them *more* clearly a geometry job, not less.

## 2. THE "100 % CALIDAD" DECAL, SQUARE ON AND CLOSE

**Partly answered by `IMG_2073.jpeg` — the burst is 44 × 61 px there against 23 × 39 in
`ref_playa_34.png`.** That was enough to measure the **word gap** (rev 47b: 1.64× the build's, applied)
and it was **not** enough for the rest.

**Four defects are now visible and none is measurable at 44 px:**

1. **The spikes.** The photograph has **many short, fine, near-uniform needle spikes** on a nearly
   circular core; the build has ~20 **long, broad, irregular** ones. We cannot count the photograph's.
2. **The burst colour.** The photograph looks a fairly **uniform deep crimson**; the build runs a
   strong **RED → ORANGE → YELLOW** gradient. Whether the photograph has *any* gradient is not
   resolvable at this size or under this white balance.
3. **The stars.** The photograph shows **several small magenta stars scattered around the decal**;
   the build has **one**. Their count and placement are not measurable.
4. **"The lettering looks off as well"** — still his open report. The letters are ~4 px tall in
   `IMG_2073`. **Still not evaluable.**

A phone photo square on to the panel from a metre or two settles all four at once.
