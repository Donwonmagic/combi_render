## 1. THE TAIL, WITH THE ENGINE LID OPEN

**Settles four things in one frame:** the hinge line and side, the open angle, whether it is
stay-held or counterbalanced, and what the bay contains.

**Why it cannot be settled from what we hold:** *no frame in this project shows the trunk open.*
`SPEC.md` §10.26 claimed `ref_side.jpg` did; rev 48 refuted that — the raised panel there is a thin
bulb-lined board whose base measures **1.78 ± 0.07 m** above ground (the drip rail), against
0.60–1.10 m for the engine lid. About 11 σ.

`TRUNK_OPEN_DEG = 52.0` and `REAR_OPEN_DEG = 64.0` are both **pose choices with zero photographic
support**, declared as such in the source and guarded to keep declaring it. This frame is the first
thing that would replace them with measurements.

**And it is the one you asked for.** You said to fill the bay as a service bay; rev 48 built the
opening and stopped short of inventing contents, because nothing in the reference set supports any.

## 2. THE "100 % CALIDAD" DECAL — SQUARE ON, AND **NOT BLOWN OUT**

**Settles:** the word gap, the spike count and character, the star count, the burst colour, and
"the lettering looks off as well" — five open items.

**Why it cannot be settled from what we hold, and this is the specific reason:** `ref_side.jpg`
**already has the resolution** — the burst is **99 × 75 px** there, 2.7× the area of any other
frame. What it does not have is **dynamic range**. Both red-bus frames are clipped in the highlights,
so the white type does not separate from the red burst at any threshold, and the star band comes back
as one merged 1499-px blob.

*A darker exposure of the same shot would do it.* Phone HDR off, or exposure pulled down a stop or
two — the decal correctly exposed, even if the rest of the frame goes dark.

## 3. THE REAR QUARTER, SQUARE ON — THE LOUVRE BLOCK

**Settles:** the block's length, which is the one dimension of it still in dispute. The build is
**0.2952 m** long against a source comment recording **0.385 m** measured off `ref_side.jpg`.

**Why it cannot be settled from what we hold:** the count (10) and the pitch (8.106 ± 0.023 px) are
now confirmed, but the two admissible frames disagree on length by 2.1×, and the one needing no
de-foreshortening is at SNR 1.3.

**Also wanted, and cheap if you are there anyway:** a **raking-light** shot — sun or a lamp low and
along the flank — of six or so slats filling the frame. The shadow length against the known pitch
gives the pressing depth directly, which is the number that would settle whether the built louvres
are deep enough.

## 4. THE SECOND RAISED PANEL, WITH THE ROOF CLOSED

**Settles:** whether the rear-roof board covers a cut opening or stands on solid roof, and its hinge
line, chord and width. **The build is one raised panel short** — both red frames show a thin
bulb-lined board at the tail that nothing in the model occupies.

**Why it cannot be settled from what we hold:** all four frames are near edge-on to it. We have its
image-plane chord and tilt and nothing else; its width across the vehicle is completely unmeasured.

**A frame with the roof CLOSED is worth more than one with it open** — the shut lines read against
the roof and settle station, chord and width at once.
