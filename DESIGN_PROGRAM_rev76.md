## §0 THE RETRACTION — READ THIS BEFORE ANY FIGURE IN THIS FILE (rule 13)

**THE ORCHESTRATOR PUBLISHED A FIGURE BELOW ITS OWN MEASUREMENT FLOOR, IN A PROGRAM WHOSE STATED
SPINE IS PUBLISHING DOUBT.** The first draft of the shortlist quoted `109.5 | 129.5 PILLARS` and called it
*"the whole thesis in one figure — the three holes are equal but the two pillars between them are
NOT, and no stylised bus drawing on earth gets that right."* **It is not a fact. It is 0.67σ.**

Recomputed from `t1_shell.py`'s own comment — *"+0.672 / +0.047 / −0.598 **+/- 0.015**"* — and its
`BAY_W = 0.5155`:

```
  pitch A  0.6250      pitch B  0.6450      asymmetry  20.0 mm
  sigma(pitch) = 0.015 * sqrt(2) = 21.2 mm ; sigma(asymmetry) = 30.0 mm
  => 20.0 +/- 30.0 mm = 0.67 sigma.  THREE EQUAL PILLARS SIT INSIDE THE MEASUREMENT.
  (the 109.5 / 129.5 are derived from BAY_CX, so their difference IS this number)
```

**And twenty lines below that comment the same file kills SPEC 10.5's bay taper for exactly this
reason** — *"a 2 mm taper is below the measurement floor and inventing one is how 10.5's version got
here."* **Found by the completeness critic, verified independently by the orchestrator.**

**WHAT TO DO:** either **re-measure the pillars DIRECTLY as edge-to-edge gaps on one frame** (common-
mode error cancels; 20 mm is 4.4 px at `ref_side.jpg`'s 220 px/m), or **draw three equal pillars** and
move the fingerprint to the door-to-bay-0 pillar, which has a stated *cause* rather than a residual.
**DO NOT print 109.5 / 129.5 as fact.**

## §1 THE SPINE — AND IT WAS RECOVERED, NOT INVENTED

**THE MODEL'S VALUE TO THIS PROGRAM IS NOT PHOTOREALISM. IT IS MEASUREMENT AND LINE.**

Three of the four top-audited concepts need the *same single missing capability* — **a line-extraction
pass** — and none of them wants a photoreal render. What the model uniquely provides is: exact
geometry, true orthographic projection, and **surface-following line and occlusion**. Nothing else in
the reference set can give those.

since** (F18 carries only "cartoon with rendered depth"). It is not a mood — **it is a render recipe,

**CONSEQUENCE FOR THE HOLDS:** F191 and F193 gate the **photoreal delivery render**. A line-and-flat-

## §3 THE SHORTLIST — audited, ranked, with the auditors' improvements grafted in

Scores are `screen /10` then `audit /4` across three adversarial lenses.

### 1. **EL COMBI — LA LOTERÍA** · 9.0 / 3.00 — *strong on all three lenses*
A 24-card lotería deck, **one black ink on warm cream**, every card a trade-catalogue **engraving of
one part of this one vehicle**: La Ventana (the serving aperture, 515.5 × 403.0 mm, r 55), La Rueda
(hubcap 274.0 mm, badge 86.9 mm), El Farol (one festoon bulb on its flex), El Mostrador, La Cortina.
**The line system is the idea**: silhouette 0.5 mm, creases 0.3 mm, hatching 0.12–0.18 mm, no black
mass wider than ~3 mm, value from hatch density alone — **and the hatch direction follows the
surface**, longitudinal on the flank, circumferential round the tyre, radial from the hubcap pole,
cross-hatched only in true occlusion. *That* is what separates an engraving from a Photoshop filter,
and it requires a normal pass — which is to say, it requires the model.
**GRAFTED FROM THE AUDIT:** purge the four borrowed archetypes (cut El Sol, El Vaso, La Bocina —
the last has no geometry, F309-retired — and replace with **El Volante**, the built steering wheel);
**put a real measured dimension on every card face** (`LA VENTANA · 515.5 × 403.0 mm · r 55`); give
each card a second line naming the archetype it displaces (`LA SOMBRA / la muerte`); write 24 dichos
in Tacombi's deadpan voice. **Ship "card zero" — one card end to end — before drawing the other 23.**

### 2. **NO LEGIBLE** · 9.0 / 3.00 — *strong on all three, and it needs NO model work*
A B0 poster on the **measured menu-strip yellow (172,144,17)** in dark red (72,46,6): a dense column
of **everything on this vehicle that could not be read**, each specimen reproduced at the actual pixel
scale and blur at which the record read it, then enlarged with the blur intact — captioned with the
project's own honest admissions. *"THE THREE LEGIBLE WORDS AVERAGE 53.6 PX PER CHARACTER… THAT IS A
CONSISTENCY, NOT A READING."* *"LA SANTA REMAINS CONSISTENT AND REMAINS UNREAD."*
Anyone who has stood before a hand-painted taqueria board they could only half read, and ordered
anyway, gets it instantly. **It reframes illegibility as intimacy rather than failure.**
**GRAFTED:** make the hero **the hole, not a sentence** — an empty rectangle of untouched stock where
the palm frond occludes the mural; derive the layout from the measurements (one photographed pixel =
one fixed physical size) rather than designing a grid; split into two posters, Playa and Nolita, and
let the Nolita one carry the payoff.
**⚠ THIS IS THE ONE THAT SHIPS UNDER THE HOLDS WITH ZERO GEOMETRY RISK.**

### 3. **THE UNCERTAINTY PLATE** · 8.5 / 3.00 — *strong on all three*
A factory-style orthographic elevation where **every dimension line confesses how it was obtained**.
Measured figures in solid ink with filled arrowheads (`2400 WHEELBASE` · `1369/1359 TRACK` ·
`515.5 × 403.0 SERVING APERTURE ×3` · `17.75 mm/m RAKE` · **`109.5 | 129.5 PILLARS`** — the three
holes are equal and the two pillars between them are *not*, which no stylised bus drawing on earth
gets right). Unmeasured figures in 40% tint with open-bracket arrowheads.
**GRAFTED:** make the uncertainty **graphic, not verbal** — draw ranges as ranges; **print the
disagreement**: the tail board is built at 38.0° and photographed at 28.0°, unresolved for seven
revisions — draw both. Invert the hierarchy so **`ENGINE: NONE. TRANSMISSION: SOLD.`** is the largest
annotation on the sheet.
**⚠ BLOCKER, RE-MEASURED AND NARROWED AT REV 76:** `front` and `rear` are `ortho=3.55`
→ **2.4406 m** vertical at 1600×1100 against `bbox top 3.132`, so they **clip** — `out/r75_front.png`
reads **43 ink pixels on the top row, 738 on the bottom**. ⚠ **BUT THE CONCEPT'S CLAIM THAT THIS
"BLOCKS ANY ELEVATION SERIES" IS TOO STRONG: `side` is `ortho=5.90` → 4.0563 m and FITS.** This plate
is a side elevation, so **it is not blocked today.** Fix front/rear before any multi-view series.

### 4. **PAPEL DE GRASA** · 9.0 / 2.67
Deli paper printed both faces in back-to-front register: **top face, the bus shut and dark in maroon
line; reverse, amber — only the light.** Dry, it is austere. **Then the food arrives**, grease wicks
through, the paper goes translucent, and the bus lights up and opens while you eat. Costs under a
cent, cannot be replicated at home, and the customer finishes the image by eating.
**GRAFTED:** engineer the spread with an oil-barrier resist rather than hoping for it; re-compose the
amber for a **blob, not a rim** (put the reveal in the lower-centre band where a taco actually sits);
**measure the wet zone on ten blank sheets with real food before drawing anything.**

## §5 THE CRITICAL PATH — ordered, cheapest high-leverage first

1. **THE LINE PASS. This is the keystone and it does not exist.**
   `grep -rln "freestyle|gpencil|use_pass_|cryptomatte" --include=*.py .` finds nothing.
   Build the **Grease Pencil Line Art** route in preference to Freestyle: it emits **editable vector
   strokes and is the only path in this tree to SVG**, which is what screen positives, flexo plates
   and die-cut cutters all want. Crease angle **30–40°, not lower** — `T1_body` is shade-smooth and a
   low angle finds the subdivision instead of the form. The mesh can take it: `STATE.md` reads
   **non-manifold edges (body) 0**, 229,489 quads to 1,040 tris.
   **Unlocks concepts 1, 3 and 4 simultaneously, and it IS the owner's recovered rev-39 sentence.**
2. **NORMAL + AO PASSES** to drive hatch direction and occlusion — the other half of that sentence.
3. **FIX THE ORTHOGRAPHIC CLIPPING ON `front`/`rear` — ⚠ BUT IT DOES NOT BLOCK `side`, AND THE
   AUDITED CONCEPT SAID IT DID.** Verified at rev 76 rather than inherited: `studio.py` sets
   `"front"` and `"rear"` to **`ortho=3.55`** → 3.55 × 1100/1600 = **2.4406 m** vertical against
   `STATE.md`'s **`bbox top 3.132`**, so both clip — and `out/r75_front.png` proves it with **43 ink
   pixels on its top row against 738 on the bottom**. **`"side"` is `ortho=5.90` → 4.0563 m and
   FITS.** Since the top concepts are side-elevation or single-object work, **the keystone view
   works today**; this fixes front/rear elevations only. Derive `ortho_scale` from the build's own
   bounds so it cannot silently regress.
4. **PROVE `T1_ALPHA`.** Transparent output is built and guarded and has been **un-run for thirteen
   revisions** — no ledger contains the string. Every frame in `out/` is RGB with no alpha, and
   `deliver.py` correctly refuses them. **One env var stands between the record and the owner's
   "different backgrounds" ruling.**
5. **`deliver.py` writes its whole 21-file package even when it refuses** — only the zip is withheld.
   A folder that looks finished, with a README claiming transparent backgrounds, exists on disk after
   a failure. F49's shape on a second script. **Fix the ordering.**
6. **`post.py` DESTROYS ALPHA** (measured: RGBA in, colortype 2 out). Optics *or* cut-out, never
   both. Undeclared. **And the whole large-format chain is 8-bit** while the render authors 16.
7. **NO 3D EXPORT PATH EXISTS.** No glTF/OBJ/USD/FBX anywhere; only an undocumented `.blend` save.
   Any external designer or animator needs this.
