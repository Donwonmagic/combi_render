# PHOTOS WANTED — rev 45

**Supersedes `PHOTOS_WANTED_rev44.md`.** Ranked. Everything here is a thing no amount of further
measuring on the frames we already hold can settle — each entry says *why it cannot*, so nobody
spends another revision trying.

**Your five uploads closed a lot.** `ref_nolita_front34.jpg` alone settled the headlamp bezel and
gave rev 45 the frame it needed for the badge; `ref_playa_34.png` — which this project had never
seen before — settled the Calidad decal's colour, gave the only unlit headlamp lens in the set, and
is the only frame anywhere showing the painted livery the model actually reproduces. Three of rev
45's five fixes come off frames you sent.

---

## 1. THE OFF SIDE — a flat-on right flank

**Unmoved at the top of this list for four revisions.** The off flank sits at 804.9 mm, graded **E**,
and **we do not know what is painted on it.** The model carries a de-mirrored variant on an
assumption made at rev 10 and never tested.

*A three-quarter view is useless here.* Stand square to it, as far back as the space allows.

## 2. ANYTHING THAT SETTLES THE ABSOLUTE ROOF HEIGHT

`H_ROOF` was retired as an accuracy target at rev 22 and never replaced. The build prints a paragraph
every single run saying the figure is unsupported. **One photograph of you standing beside it, plus
your height, closes an eleven-revision hole.**

## 3. THE NOSE BADGE, SQUARE ON — new, rev 45, and it is cheap

Stand directly in front of the bus, phone at badge height, fill the frame with the badge.

**Why nothing we hold can do this.** `ref_workshop.jpg` shows the emblem at 92 px vertical — the
largest in the set — and it is still not enough, because reading angles off a three-quarter view of a
circle requires un-squashing it, and that needs the ring's axis ratio. The two independent fits
available disagree by **10 %** (§10.107's 0.687 against rev 45's 0.764), which is several degrees on
every angle — the whole size of the thing anyone would be trying to measure. **A square-on frame has
no squash to undo.**

Rev 45 fixed why the badge rendered as a clock face (it was sunk into the bodywork) and deliberately
did **not** touch the drawing, because this frame does not exist yet.

## 4. THE CAB INTERIOR, SQUARE ON THROUGH THE WINDSCREEN — new at rev 44b, still open

The cab is **class 4**: it was built at rev 44, it is type-correct 1963 T1, and **no frame in this
project resolves it**. It is placed off members that are fixed, so it cannot drift independently of
the shell — but that is a coupling, not a measurement.

Related and open: the driving position is 622 mm from the seat back to the hub, about 150 mm more
reach than a T1 driver has.

## 5. THE ROOF SIGN BOARD, AND WHICH ONE YOU WANT — new, rev 45

The build paints the raised board as a **flower mural with menu strips down its edges**. Every
photograph we hold — `ref_nolita_doorshut.jpg`, `ref_nolita_front34b.jpg` — shows a **hand-chalked
BLACKBOARD** in a cream frame, TACOMBI across the top, BIENVENIDOS down the side.

**This is a question before it is a photo request** (Q5 of `rev45_ba.png`). If you want the
blackboard, a straight-on frame of it with the writing legible would let it be reproduced rather than
invented.

## AND ONE THING THAT IS A QUESTION, NOT A PHOTOGRAPH

**The paint reads too pale, and it is the studio, not the paint.** Measured against your own
photographs — four of them, normalised so exposure and lighting colour cancel — the red renders
green-over-red **0.455** against **0.223 ± 0.066**, which is 3.5 σ. The albedo is **right** (0.250).
About half the excess is the white cyclorama's own reflection in the paint, and the same rig is why
the bus has no contact shadow on the ground.

**Softening the studio would make the paint read as it does in your photographs and would cost the
catalogue-clean white background you supplied as the bar.** That is your call, not a measurement.
It is **Q6** of `rev45_ba.png`.
